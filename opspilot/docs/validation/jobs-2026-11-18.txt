## Regression found by CI

The first two CI runs failed in the queue suite (locally: always green). Cause: claims
compared `run_at` against the *application* clock while `run_at` could be written by
the *database* default `now()`, so any skew between the two clocks made a due job look
premature. Fixed by keeping both sides on the database clock (`now()`,
`make_interval(secs => …)`), including retry scheduling; tests that insert rows directly
now pass an explicit past `run_at` instead of relying on the default. A new test
("never claims a job whose run time is in the future, on the database clock") pins the
behaviour. 29/29 after the fix, three consecutive runs.
